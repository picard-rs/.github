### Actor-based concurrency for Rust. Continuing what Riker started.

Welcome to **Picard**, a community-driven effort to maintain, modernize, and advance actor-based programming in Rust.

Picard builds upon [Riker](https://github.com/riker-rs/riker), an actor framework that brought powerful ideas around concurrency, message passing, and fault tolerance to the Rust ecosystem.

## Our Story

Picard is a continuation of Riker.

Riker's original maintainers and contributors built a strong foundation, but development eventually slowed.
Rather than see that work remain dormant, we created Picard to give the project a new home and renewed momentum.

We are deeply grateful to everyone who contributed to Riker, especially its original creator and maintainer.
Their dedication, ideas, and engineering made this project possible.

Maintaining open-source software takes time and energy, and we recognize that priorities and circumstances change.
Picard is not a criticism of the original maintainers, but a tribute to their work and an effort to carry it forward.

**Different name. Shared history. A new chapter.**

## What We're Working Toward

- **Active maintenance** — Keeping the framework healthy, addressing issues, and supporting modern Rust.
- **Stability and reliability** — Strengthening the foundations of actor-based concurrency.
- **Thoughtful evolution** — Improving APIs, performance, and developer experience without losing sight of Riker's original philosophy.
- **Open collaboration** — Building a welcoming community where contributions and ideas can shape the project's future.
- **Long-term sustainability** — Working toward a project that can thrive beyond any single maintainer.
